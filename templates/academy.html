{# How it works #}
How The Academy Works

Each new season, {{ 3 + (gs.managed_club.academy_quality or 2) }}–{{ 5 + (gs.managed_club.academy_quality or 2) }} youngsters join the academy, aged 15–17.

Players at 17+ can be promoted to the first-team squad. Those unlikely to develop are best released to free space for the next intake.

Youngsters start with a low current ability (shown in stars) but the exciting figure is their potential. They develop towards it each season — better training facilities accelerate this.

Players still in the academy at 19 with an expired contract will leave automatically. Use the Contracts page to offer extensions.
